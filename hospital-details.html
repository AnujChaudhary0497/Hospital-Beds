OVERVIEW

About Apollo Hospitals

Apollo Hospitals, Indraprastha is one of India's foremost quaternary care hospitals, part of the Apollo Hospitals Group – Asia's largest integrated healthcare enterprise. Since its founding in 1996, it has been a pioneer in clinical excellence, medical education, and patient-centred care across more than 50 clinical specialties.

The hospital's 710-bed capacity is matched by a specialist workforce of over 350 senior consultants and a team of 4,000+ clinical staff. Apollo Delhi is the first hospital in South Asia to perform robot-assisted cardiac surgery and has conducted over 16,000 cardiac procedures.

Backed by NABH, JCI, and ISO 9001:2015 accreditations, the hospital maintains clinical protocols benchmarked against global standards, with a commitment to zero preventable infections and continuous quality improvement.
