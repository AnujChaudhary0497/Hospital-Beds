ABOUT HOSPITAL BEDS

India’s Most Trusted
Healthcare Marketplace

Founded in 2019, Hospital Beds was built with a single belief: finding the right hospital shouldn’t be harder than recovering from an illness. We’ve seen families scramble in medical emergencies, patients blindsided by costs, and caregivers overwhelmed by choices.

We built Hospital Beds to change that. Today, we are India’s largest healthcare discovery platform, connecting patients with 2,846+ verified hospitals, 12,419+ specialist doctors, and hundreds of diagnostic labs — all searchable, comparable, and bookable in minutes.

From emergency bed availability to complex surgical packages, from routine blood tests to advanced cancer care — Hospital Beds is the one platform that handles it all, with full price transparency and zero middlemen.

OUR MISSION

Democratising Access to Quality Healthcare

Our mission is to eliminate the information asymmetry that exists in Indian healthcare. We want every patient – regardless of their location, income, or health literacy – to be able to find the best hospital for their needs, understand what it will cost, and book it instantly.

We do this by building technology that puts patients first, by verifying every hospital and every price, and by providing 24/7 support for every booking.

OUR VISION

A Healthier India, One Booking at a Time

We envision a future where no Indian patient ever waits hours in a hospital corridor, overpays for a procedure, or drives past a better, closer hospital simply because they didn’t know it existed.

By 2030, we aim to cover every tier-2 and tier-3 city in India, connecting 10 million patients annually with the right care at the right price.
